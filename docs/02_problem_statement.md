# Problem Statement & Market Gap Analysis

## Problem Statement

> **Target User:** Young adults and students (ages 18–28) who experience impulsive, absentminded social media use.  
> **Difficulty:** Mindless "doomscrolling" and rapid short-form content consumption (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) lead to unintended time loss, focus fragmentation, and post-scrolling guilt.  
> **Desired Improvement:** A non-punitive, friction-based intervention that detects fast mindless scrolling, interrupts the automatic feedback loop with mindful pauses and intention prompts, and encourages habit reflection without shaming the user.

---

## Analysis of Existing Alternatives

| Tool / Alternative | Mechanism | Primary Limitations & Failure Modes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Google Digital Wellbeing / Apple Screen Time** | Rigid daily time limits per app (e.g. 30 mins on Instagram). | **High Bypass Rate:** Offers instant "Ignore limit for 15 mins" or "Ignore for today" buttons, creating an automatic bypass habit. |
| **Strict App Blockers (Freedom, Opal, Lock Me Out)** | Complete app lockout or PIN/password shields. | **High User Friction:** Overly punitive; causes frustration when users legitimately need an app for communication or quick tasks, leading users to uninstall the blocker. |
| **Standard Pomodoro Timers (Forest, Minimalist Timers)** | Fixed countdown timer for work sessions. | **Passive Detection:** Fails to actively detect or intervene when a user unconsciously breaks focus and opens a distracting app. |
| **Built-in App Time Alerts (Instagram/TikTok Take a Break)** | Fixed timer alert after continuous usage (e.g., 20 mins). | **Static & Low Visibility:** Easy to dismiss with one tap; does not measure scroll velocity or prompt active intention setting. |

---

## The Gap Addressed by ScrollBrake

**ScrollBrake** fills the critical market gap between passive tracking and extreme app lockouts by introducing **Real-Time Mindful Speed Bumps**:

1. **Velocity-Based Intervention:** Rather than enforcing rigid hourly caps, ScrollBrake uses on-device scroll velocity classification to detect rapid, mindless feed flings in real-time.
2. **Intentional Speed Bumps:** When fast scrolling is detected, ScrollBrake overlays a non-intrusive 5-second breathing pause and session intention prompt, shifting the user from system-1 automatic scrolling to system-2 conscious choice.
3. **Compassionate Gamification:** ScrollBrake replaces shame-inducing red time graphs with **Attention Currency** and streak mechanics, rewarding conscious pauses and completed focus sessions.
