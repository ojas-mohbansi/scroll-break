## 5. ANDROID ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE API DISCLOSURE

Scroll Brake utilizes the Android `AccessibilityService` API to provide its core digital wellbeing features. In full transparency, here is exactly how the Accessibility Service is utilized and safeguarded:

### What the Accessibility Service Does:
1. **App & Screen Detection:** Detects when restricted apps or websites are currently active on your screen to enforce your custom Focus Mode blocking rules and intervention prompts.
2. **Scroll Counting:** Measures scroll events when Smart Scroll Intervention is active to recognize rapid doom-scrolling patterns and suggest mindful stopping points.
3. **Focus Enforcement:** Automatically returns you to the home screen if you attempt to open a restricted app during an active Focus Mode session.

### Privacy Safeguards:
* **No Screen Reading or Logging:** The Accessibility Service **does not** read, collect, or store screen text, personal messages, banking details, passwords, or keyboard inputs.
* **No Screenshots or External Transmission:** No screen images or text transcripts are ever captured, stored externally, or uploaded.
* **Local Processing:** All accessibility checks occur instantaneously and entirely on your device hardware.
